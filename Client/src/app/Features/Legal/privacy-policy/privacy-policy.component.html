Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ lastUpdated }}

1. What we collect

  • Account information: name, email, clinic name, optional profile photo.
  • Patient records entered by your clinic: intake forms, session notes, session audio, and generated reports.
  • Usage data: how the platform is used, for support and reliability purposes.

2. How we use it

Patient and session data is used to operate the service for your clinic: generating reports, transcribing sessions, and enabling search across your own patients' history through the query agent.

Separately, and only in anonymized, aggregated form, session data helps us improve how the system structures and retrieves information — for example, refining how session text is segmented for accurate search. This analysis does not identify individual patients, and identifiable patient records are never used to train AI models.

3. Storage and security

Data is stored in encrypted infrastructure, with session data indexed for semantic search to support the query agent. Access to a clinic's patient records is restricted to that clinic's authorized doctors and staff.

4. Third-party processing

Some processing, such as speech-to-text transcription and AI-assisted drafting, is performed by third-party AI providers. These providers process data solely to perform the requested task and are not authorized to use it for their own purposes.

5. Data retention

Patient and session data is retained for as long as your clinic's account is active, so you can access historical records. You may request deletion of your clinic's data by contacting us.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you and your patients may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data. Clinics are responsible for handling patient-facing privacy requests; contact us for assistance with data stored on PhysioAssist.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@physioassist.app.
