1. Who this applies to

These Terms govern use of PhysioAssist by physiotherapy clinics, doctors, and staff accounts ("you") who register a clinic on the platform. By creating an account you confirm you are authorized to register your clinic and to accept these Terms on its behalf.

2. Free plan limits

The free plan is provided so clinics can evaluate PhysioAssist with real patients before committing to a paid plan. It includes the following limits:

  • Session audio recordings are limited to 1 minute per session.
  • Up to 10 active patients per clinic.

Paid plans remove these limits and unlock additional capabilities as they become available, including longer session recordings and AI-assisted recommendations.

3. How patient data is used

Session and patient data you enter is stored to provide the service to your clinic: generating reports, transcribing sessions, and allowing you to search your own patients' records.

We also use patient data, in anonymized and aggregated form only, to improve how the system organizes and retrieves information internally (for example, how session text is broken into segments for search). This process does not identify individual patients, and no individually identifiable patient data is used to train any AI model.

4. Your responsibility as a clinic

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required from your patients for their information to be processed through PhysioAssist, in accordance with applicable data protection law in your jurisdiction. You should inform patients that their clinical records are stored and processed digitally, including through AI-assisted tools.

5. Account and data access

Each clinic's data is only accessible to that clinic's registered doctors and staff. We do not share patient-identifiable data with other clinics or third parties outside of the service providers necessary to operate PhysioAssist (e.g. hosting and AI processing providers).

6. Changes to these Terms

We may update these Terms as the product evolves, particularly as new paid features are introduced. Continued use of PhysioAssist after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised Terms.

7. Contact

Questions about these Terms can be sent to support@physioassist.app.
